# Home Assistant

Home Assistant runs my home automation: lighting, blinds, and a presence simulation that runs while the house is empty. It's deployed as its own VM on Proxmox, alongside the media automation stack.

- [My Home Assistant Setup](/docs/home-server/home-assistant/my-setup): the Proxmox host, the HAOS VM, and the Zigbee and EnOcean hardware passed through to it.
- [Hardware](/docs/home-server/home-assistant/hardware): the Zigbee and EnOcean USB dongles and the blind actuators they drive.
- [Home Presence Simulation](/docs/home-server/home-assistant/presence-simulation): a PyScript-based lighting schedule that runs while the house is empty, built to look less mechanical than a replay of past light history.
